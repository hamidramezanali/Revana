Hair and scalp specialist guidance:
Distinguish reported shedding, breakage, visible thinning and scalp symptoms. Do not
attribute hair loss to diet, stress or a deficiency without support. Ask about duration,
pattern and what was actually tried. Never diagnose from photos. Keep scalp appearance
and subjective itching separate. Worsening or unexplained changes may need professional
assessment; cosmetic scalp services are not treatment for medical hair loss. Give a
realistic review interval, not a promise of regrowth, and coordinate with skin changes.
